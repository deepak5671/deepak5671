### UPSC AI · Current affairs, notes, and MCQs

> **A full-stack platform for Civil Services aspirants.** It collects current-affairs news and uses Google Gemini to turn articles into exam-ready study material.

- Gathers news from **9+ major sources** and turns each article into structured notes, **MCQs, and a Mains question with model-answer points**.
- Builds an **AI-generated daily digest** and tags notes by paper (**GS1–GS4, Prelims, Essay**).
- Uses **JWT authentication** with bcrypt password hashing and an Express REST API (`/api/auth`, `/api/notes`, `/api/articles`) backed by **MongoDB**.
- Falls back to **localStorage** when the backend is unreachable, so saved work survives outages.
- Includes an AI study assistant, a bookmarking system, and **PDF export** of notes.

`React 19` `Vite` `Express 5` `MongoDB / Mongoose` `Gemini 2.5 Flash` `JWT`

### SakuraMart · Spring Boot e-commerce platform

> **A complete online store with separate customer and admin experiences.** It is built on a layered Spring Boot architecture: controllers, services, repositories, and JPA entities.

- **Customers** can browse and search products by category, manage a cart, check out with a delivery address, and track their orders.
- **Admins** manage products, categories, users, and other admins. They can search orders and update order status.
- Orders move through a **7-stage lifecycle**: In Progress → Received → Packed → Out for Delivery → Delivered, plus Cancelled and Success.
- **Spring Security** handles role-based access, with custom success and failure handlers and an account status toggle.
- The **forgot/reset password flow** sends an emailed reset link through Spring Mail (SMTP). Product images are uploaded through multipart forms.

`Java 17` `Spring Boot` `Spring Security` `Spring Data JPA` `Thymeleaf` `Bootstrap` `MySQL`
